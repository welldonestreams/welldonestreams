Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026. This policy describes the public Well Done Streams website and its media-request and status-lookup features.

Information we process

We process information you choose to provide when requesting access, making a media request, or checking a request status. This can include an email address, account/profile information supplied by an authentication provider, and the details of the request. Our hosting and security providers also receive routine technical information such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps in server or security logs.

Cookies and local storage

The website uses a small browser cookie to remember a poll vote and may use browser storage for appearance or interface preferences. It does not use advertising cookies, cross-site behavioral advertising, or third-party analytics trackers.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate and secure the service: providing access, processing and displaying media requests, answering status lookups, preventing duplicate votes or abuse, and maintaining the service. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Service providers and sharing

Information is shared only with providers needed to run the service, such as the hosting, security, authentication, and media-request systems used to deliver it. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to protect the service, users, or legal rights.

Retention and deletion requests

We keep request and account records for as long as reasonably needed to operate the service, resolve support issues, and protect against abuse. You may ask to access, correct, or delete information associated with your email address by contacting us. Some records may need to be retained where required for security, legal, or operational reasons.

Security and children

We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. The service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. The current version will be posted here. For privacy questions or requests, email support@welldonestreams.com.
